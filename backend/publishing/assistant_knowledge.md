## What moves the price
Up: more user roles, payments, mobile money, integrations with other systems, offline use, moving old data, custom design, tight deadlines, several languages.
Down: a clear first version, ready text and images, using proven tools, building in phases.
A tight budget is workable: start with the part that matters most, then grow it in phases, as long as the first phase is at least TSh 500,000.

## Costs that come on top
Domains, hosting, app store fees, payment-provider fees, and paid tools or AI usage are separate. Hosting and maintenance are optional monthly or yearly arrangements agreed in the quote.
